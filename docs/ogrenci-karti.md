# Öğrenci kartı (guidance teacher)

One page per student: how they stand against the **Türkiye average** of each exam, how steady they are, and what else the school knows about them (etüts, absences, weak topics). It is a teacher-only page (`ogrencikarti`), not a menu item: it opens from a student's name (Deneme panosu, Etüt etkisi, and Sıralamalar for students who are not in the notebook) and marks **Öğrenciler** in the menu. A search box at the top (name or school number) opens any student directly; a button leads back to the page it was opened from.

## Where the data comes from

Nothing is added, stored or uploaded; the page only reads and caches in memory (`src/analiz.js`, `ANALIZ`; state in `EK.okKim`, `EK.okOturum`, `EK.okGeri`).

- **Exams and nets:** Sıralamalar' lists through `DPANO.temel`, with Deneme panosu's measures: TYT, and AYT per alan (SAY, EA, SÖZ) for the students who took the alan's tests. A student is identified by school number.
- **Türkiye average:** Türkiye geneli, `TURKIYE.ortalamalar`. An exam is matched by its name (the match that dates a Türkiye list); the Kolej list comes first. Never the school mean.
- **Etüt:** the merged students of Öğrenci raporları (`OGRENCI_RAPOR.temelOgrenciler`). **Yoklama:** its students matched by name and class. **Konu Takibi:** the student's blocks, by school number. **Hedef:** the class / şube target of Deneme ortalaması (`denemeOrtHedefi`).
- Every source is optional; a part without its source says so ("—", "Etüt kaydı yok").

## Definitions

- **Sapma:** the student's net minus the Türkiye average of that exam and session (TYT total; AYT per alan). The student's net counts the same subjects as the Türkiye total (Ek Felsefe stands in for Din Kültürü). An exam with neither a Türkiye list nor a tahmini average (below) has no sapma; the card says "Bu denemenin Türkiye ortalaması yok" and the chart still draws the student's net.
- **Tahmini Türkiye ortalaması:** for an exam of a measure that has no Türkiye list, the school mean of the exam minus the **median** of (school mean − Türkiye average) over the exams of the same measure that have both; it needs at least one such exam, otherwise there is none (AYT with no list holding its subjects). The estimate is used for sapma / beklenen / beklenenden / istikrar and the target path of that exam, flagged `tahmini: true`. Cards say "tahmini Türkiye ortalaması (okul ortalamasından)"; on the chart the point is hollow and the Türkiye segment to it lighter. The student's net there is her whole net (the list that would say which subjects the Türkiye total counts is missing).
- **Okul sürprizi:** on an exam with a Türkiye list, the school's gap (school mean − Türkiye average) minus its usual gap (median over the *other* exams with a list; at least one other is needed). Under the chart one line says it for the latest such exam: "Okul ortalaması 27-YKS-1205 denemesinde Türkiye’ye göre her zamankinden ▲3,3 net iyi" (*kötü* when below). `ANALIZ.okulSurprizi(measure)` returns it for every exam, for other pages.
- **Hedef yolu** (the personal target, always defined once she has one exam with a Türkiye average): g0 is her sapma at her first exam with a Türkiye average (real or tahmini); the target net of an exam is its Türkiye average + the target gap, kept below the measure's maximum (**TYT 120, AYT SAY / EA / SÖZ 80**, the same `ust` as Deneme panosu; the cap is maximum − 0,5). At her k-th later exam the gap is the previous (capped) gap + **adım × min(1, (maksimum − önceki hedef) / (maksimum / 2))**: adım is +1 net for TYT and +0,5 for each AYT measure, in full up to the middle of the scale, shrinking toward 0 near the maximum, so the closer to 120 the harder the effort shows and the target never reaches it. Card **Hedef · son deneme**: the target net, "Türkiye +x" and ▲/▼ the distance of her net to it. Chart: its own orange dashed line. The şube / sınıf target of Deneme ortalaması, when set, stays as a thin line (**Şube hedefi** / **Sınıf hedefi**) and its own card.
- **Deneme aralığı:** her oturum iki haftada bir (the school holds TYT and AYT in alternate weeks), `DENEME_ARALIGI_GUN = 14`. The exams still to come before YKS are **m = floor((YKS tarihi − son deneme) / 14)**, at most 30; they are not estimated from her own past dates.
- **Gidişat:** a bounded, robust trend that does not doom her for one bad exam or for moving inside her usual range. It works in the success-ratio space: **r = logit(net / maksimum) − logit(Türkiye / maksimum)** (both ratios kept between 0,02 and 0,98; Türkiye real or tahmini). **Level** = the median of r over her last up to 5 exams. **Slope** = Theil–Sen (the median of the slopes between every pair of exams, r against the exam order) over all her exams that have a Türkiye value; when |slope| ≤ MAD(r) / √n it is noise and the slope is 0 (the card then says "aralığında dalgalanıyor (≈ 82–109)", her lowest and highest net of the last 5). To YKS: **r_YKS = level + slope × Σ 0,85^j** (j = 1 … m), so a real trend fades and plateaus; **net at YKS = maksimum × sigmoid(r_YKS + logit(son Türkiye / maksimum))**, always between 0 and the maximum. At least 3 exams with a Türkiye value, otherwise "Gidişat için Türkiye ortalamalı en az 3 deneme gerekli". The hedef yolu at YKS steps once for each of the same m exams to come, with the shrinking step. Under the chart: "Gidişat · YKS’de ~108 net" (and "aralığında dalgalanıyor (≈ 97–109)" when the slope is 0), and "Gidişat hedefin 6,0 net altında / üstünde · hedef yolu YKS’de 118 net".
- **Aralık:** **Son 1 ay · Son 3 ay · Tümü** (default Tümü), as on a stock chart, anchored on her latest exam: the earliest of her exams within that many calendar months of it (`DPANO.ayGeri`) → the latest. A range with fewer than 2 of her exams is off (Tümü takes over). Dragging on the chart (the shared `GRAFIK_OLCUM`: the readout "18 Ağu → 23 Eki · 99,8 → 106,8 net ▲+7,0") and releasing makes the two exams the range (one more pressed button names it; Tümü returns). The range drives **Aralıkta değişim** (change, number of exams, change per exam = change ÷ (exams − 1)) and the **Dersler** table: Ders | Başta | Sonda | Değişim | Deneme başına | Türkiye’ye göre (son), from her first exam of the range to the last (the Türkiye column is against the last exam's lesson averages). The chart: the shorter ranges show only the exams of the range.
- **Beklenen:** the exam's Türkiye average plus the mean sapma over the student's previous up to 5 exams that have one; it needs at least 2. **Beklenenden:** the net above (▲) or below (▼) it. Fewer than 2 earlier exams: "En az 3 deneme gerekli".
- **İstikrar:** the standard deviation (of the whole group of values) of sapma over the last up to 6 exams that have one, at least 3. Below 3 net *Çok istikrarlı*, below 6 *İstikrarlı*, below 10 *Dalgalı*, otherwise *Çok dalgalı*.
- **Etüt / devamsızlık:** the last 30 days (today and the 29 days before). A missed day follows Yoklama's rule (3 or more missed lessons; *tam gün* when no lesson was attended, else *yarım gün*).
- **Zayıf konular:** Konu Takibi topics whose "zorluğa göre" is −1 question or less, the worst first, at most 5.

## The page, top to bottom

Header (name, şube, alan, school number), the student search and the TYT / AYT switch, the cards (**Son net** with the place in the school, **Türkiye'ye göre**, **Beklenenden**, **İstikrar**, **Hedef · son deneme**, **Aralıkta değişim**, and **Şube / Sınıf hedefi** with the distance when one is set), the chart with the **Aralık** buttons (the student's nets on the real dates, the school mean dotted, the Türkiye average dashed, the hedef yolu orange dashed, the YKS column with the gidişat and hedef yolu values), two lines under it (gidişat against the target path; okul sürprizi), **Dersler**, **Zayıf konular**, **Etütler (son 30 gün)** and **Devamsızlık (son 30 gün)**.

**The chart.** The x-axis is the order of the exams: every exam of the measure in the range has a slot of the same width, dated under its point ("23 Eki"; when the dates would collide every n-th, always the first and the last), so two exams a few days apart are not a jump. The y-axis ends at the measure's maximum (the top tick is 120 for TYT, 80 for AYT). Every line is a straight segment over its own points (the student's too, whatever Köşeli / Yumuşak says here); the estimated Türkiye point is one hollow marker. When the range ends at her last exam and there is a YKS date, one small separated column **YKS · 19 Haz** stands after a gap, with two end markers: **Gidişat** (blue) and **Hedef yolu** (orange), each with its value and joined to its last value by a short dashed segment; nothing runs across the months in between. The lines are named in the right margin, in the order of their values, on a phone with shorter names (Okul, Türkiye).

## For other pages

`ANALIZ` is the shared base: `kaynak(bugun)` (cached by a signature of the school lists, the Türkiye, etüt, Yoklama and Konu Takibi stamps, the roster and the day) gives `olcular[measure] = {sinavlar, ogr: Map(no → series), okulFarki, okul, adim, ust}`; an exam is `{id, ad, tarih, g, n, okulOrt, trOrt, trVar, trTahmini, tr, tahmini}` (`trOrt` stays the real average or null; `tr` is the one the figures use, real or tahmini), each series point `{exam, ad, tarih, g, net, okulOrt, n, sira, trOrt, tr, tahmini, sapma, beklenen, beklenenden, hedefK, hedefGap, hedefNet}`; `kart(no, measure, bugun, aralik)` is one student's card (`aralik`: `'tumu'` default, `'1ay'`, `'3ay'` or `{bas, bit}` exam ids; adds `ust` (the measure's maximum), `hedefYol`, `aralik`, `degisim`, `gidisat`, `hedefYks`, `gidisatHedef`, `yks`, `okul`; `dersler` rows are `{kod, ad, soru, bas, son, degisim, basina, trOrt, trFark}`); `okulSurprizi(measure)`, `istikrar(series)`, `turet(points, adim, ust)`, `gidisat(points, yksGunu, ust)` (`var`, `n`, `egim`, `dalgalanir`, `aralik`, `seviye`, `yks: {g, m, r, gap, net}`, `cizgi`), `hedefYks(points, adim, yksGunu, ust)`, `kalanSinav(sonGun, yksGunu)`, `DENEME_ARALIGI_GUN`, `aralikSec`, `etutOzeti`, `devamsizlik`, `zayifKonular` are pure; `cizim(card, width)` returns the chart's SVG with what was drawn. Everything that existed before stays under its name. A name that should open the card is `ANALIZ.adDugmesi(no, name)` (a button with `data-ogrenci-karti`; one delegated handler opens it).
