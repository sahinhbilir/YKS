# Öğretmen raporu

**Denemeler ▾ → Öğretmen raporu** (rehber öğretmen only) gives every subject teacher one section: how the students they teach are doing in their subject against students across Türkiye, exam by exam, and which topics of the subject go well and which do not, measured on the same questions.

## Who reads it

The rehber öğretmen prints it (**Yazdır / PDF**) and hands each teacher their own sheets. The reader is a subject teacher who has never opened the app, does not know where the numbers come from or how they are worked out, and has no statistics vocabulary. So the PDF is the product, and it has to explain itself:

- every figure sits under a plain label and a line saying what it is (*Öğrencileriniz 14,3 net — 72 öğrencinin ortalaması*);
- every picture carries its own key and title, right beside it;
- the sheets say whom they cover, where the data comes from and how to read a sign and a colour (**Bu raporu okurken**);
- nothing is a symbol to decode: a topic behind on three exams running says *Son 3 denemede geride*, a difference too small to trust says *fark küçük; tesadüf olabilir*;
- no statistical term (z, anlamlılık, tasarım etkisi …) appears outside the folded appendix **Hesaplama ayrıntıları ve ham veriler**, where the formulas and every raw number stay available so that nothing is hidden. The appendix is printed only when it is open.

## What it never does

- **No class comparisons.** A teacher's students are the students of all the classes the teacher teaches the subject in, taken together. A class is never shown on its own and classes are never ranked against each other (the class names appear only to say whom the section covers).
- **No students.** No student is named, listed or ranked.
- **The only place shown** is the whole school's among Türkiye's schools, as the publisher's list prints it.
- **No teacher comparisons.** Each section is about one teacher; there is no league table.
- **No etüts.** The report is about the lessons a teacher gives in class. An etüt is a one-to-one session after school and is not part of it (Etütler and Etüt etkisi are the pages for those). The page does not read the etüt report or the student base at all.

## Where the data comes from

- **Teachers, subjects, classes:** the classes' weekly timetables in **Etüt planı** (`programlar`: day, time, lesson, teacher). A lesson is mapped to its exam subjects (`DERSLER`): Matematik → TYT and AYT Matematik; Türk dili ve edebiyatı → TYT Türkçe and AYT Edebiyat; Tarih → TYT Tarih, AYT Tarih-1 and -2; and so on. Lessons with no exam subject (Beden eğitimi, Rehberlik …) are left out. No timetable → the page says so and links to Etüt planı.
- **TYT or AYT, class by class:** the timetable decides. In a class, a lesson whose name gives its session (*TYT MATEMATİK*, *AYT MATEMATİK*) or is Türkçe (TYT by its name) takes that session's exam subject alone; a lesson that names none (*MATEMATİK*, *GEOMETRİ*, *FİZİK*, *TÜRK DİLİ VE EDEBİYATI*) takes the exam subjects no such lesson took. So:
  - TYT and AYT Matematik split between two teachers where the timetable splits them;
  - Geometri, Fizik and the other mixed lessons are TYT and AYT;
  - in an EA class with a Türkçe lesson, Türk dili ve edebiyatı is AYT Edebiyat; alone (other classes) it is TYT Türkçe and AYT Edebiyat, and a block lists only the classes that had data, so an SAY class does not appear under AYT Edebiyat.

  A subject a teacher teaches in one session only says so under their name: *Matematik (AYT) · 12/C*.
- **Nets:** the school's exam lists (Sıralamalar, through `ANALIZ.kaynak()`).
- **Türkiye average:** the publisher's Genel Net from the Türkiye geneli lists.
- **A kazanım with no topic is its own row.** Konu Takibi's built-in kazanım → topic map covers the 9th and 10th grade codes and Türkçe; an 11th or 12th grade kazanım (most of AYT) has a topic only if the teacher mapped it. The report asks `ogrenciFark` for `kazanim: true`, so such a kazanım is a row under its own name and every question of every exam is in the table. The 10th–12th grade **maths** topics (Polinomlar … Türev, İntegral, Çemberin Analitik İncelenmesi) are in the built-in map by their number in the **earlier** programme (`MEB_MAT`, read from the code whatever its prefix), which this year's 12th grade follows, so its AYT Matematik and Geometri group by konu. The 9th–11th grades follow the **new** programme, whose codes start with the ders in full (`MAT.11.1.1`) and whose numbers mean other topics: those are never read from the earlier list; an 11th grade code takes the unit that lists it in the app's curriculum (`MUFREDAT_2026`), and a code with no list stays a kazanım row. The other lessons of the earlier programme (Fizik, Kimya, Biyoloji, Tarih and İnkılap Tarihi, Çağdaş Türk ve Dünya Tarihi, Coğrafya, Din Kültürü, Felsefe, Mantık, Psikoloji, Sosyoloji, Türk Dili ve Edebiyatı) are in `ESKI`: unit names by grade.unit, and topic names by grade.unit.topic where a unit is broad (Fizik 11–12, Biyoloji İnsan Fizyolojisi). The ders comes from the publisher's course code; within Tarih and the Felsefe grubu, from the code's own prefix (ÇTD, MAN, PS, SOS). These lists were written from the 2018 programmes, not read from the publisher's files: a number not listed stays a kazanım row. (Before, these were dropped: an AYT Matematik block showed only the few 9th–10th grade questions of one exam, which read as TYT topics.)
- **Topics:** Konu Takibi: the students' kazanım reports, the exam summary (question → kazanım) and the publisher's **Performans analizi** (Türkiye's success rate per question). Without the Performans analizi the grid is replaced by a one-line note saying what to add.

Each teacher's subject is split by grade (an exam belongs to one grade) and by exam subject: one block per *TYT Matematik · 12. sınıf*, *AYT Matematik · 12. sınıf* …

**Sınıf düzeyi** (2026-11-19) is a row of buttons, one a grade (*12. sınıf*, *11. sınıf* …), the highest chosen first and one always chosen: there is no *Bütün düzeyler*. The page shows that grade's teachers, the classes of that grade under their names, its blocks, and the grade in the page title (also in print and on the PDF). The student's report (Türkiye karşılaştırması, öğrenci) has the same buttons.

## A block

Simplified on 2026-11-18 so that it reads from colour and shape alone: no difference box, no school place, no İlk 100 yardstick, no summary sentence, no bar panel, no numbers in the topic squares. Top to bottom, the same on screen and on paper:

1. **What it covers:** the title (*TYT Matematik · 12. sınıf*) and one line: the classes and the number of exams (*12/A, 12/B, 12/C · 12 deneme*).
**Türkiye on this page (2026-11-19)** is the publisher's whole network, the same pupils the topic squares are measured against: its mean net in a ders comes from that exam's **Performans analizi** in Konu Takibi (`KONU_TAKIBI.agNet`): Σ (p − w / 4), p each question's network-wide success rate, w its wrong share (p's remainder split as the file's top and bottom 27% groups split it into wrong and blank). Checked on 27-YKS-1202: TYT Matematik 11,67 (the İlk 100 list's Genel Net 11,87), AYT Geometri 0,90 (the list: 3,11, the top schools' level). The İlk 100 list's figure is no longer the line (kept as `ilk100`); an exam without a Performans analizi, or with one added before 2026-11-19 (no wrong shares; Konu Takibi → Kaynaklar says to add it again), has no Türkiye value. The list still gives **the school's İlk 100 place** (`sira`, when the Türkiye geneli page follows a school): under each exam's name on the chart, with ▲ green / ▼ red against the exam before (outside the list: *100+*, counted as 101st), and beside where the students stand *Sıralama yükseldi / düştü / aynı*. Teachers' pages only: a student's sheet has no school place.

2. **Two numbers and two words** (`skorHtml`, `durumu`), for the newest exam that has a Türkiye list (a newer exam without one is skipped, never filled in; its name is written under the words): *Öğrencileriniz* (red or green) beside *Türkiye*, in nets, a pill *▲ Türkiye’nin üstünde* (green) / *▼ Türkiye’nin altında* (red) / *■ Türkiye ile aynı*, and beside it how the gap moved since the exam before, in points of the test: behind and *Fark daralıyor* green, *Fark açılıyor* red; ahead and *Fark daralıyor* red, *Fark açılıyor* green; under 1 point *Fark değişmedi*. The question count (`soruSayisi`, used by the gap's move and the chart's scale): the publisher's Sınav özeti when Konu Takibi has it (one entry a question), else the YKS count (TYT Türkçe 40, Tarih/Coğrafya/Felsefe/Din 5, Matematik 30, Geometri 10, Fizik 7, Kimya 7, Biyoloji 6; AYT Edebiyat 24, Tarih-1 10, Coğrafya-1 6, Tarih-2 11, Coğrafya-2 11, Felsefe grubu 12, Din 6, Matematik 30, Geometri 10, Fizik 14, Kimya 13, Biyoloji 13), or the most answers a student of the list gave when that is above it or well under it (a lower grade's shorter test). No Türkiye average at all: no headline.
3. **Net chart**, its key directly above it, one picture: the students' mean (solid, the accent blue, every value written on its point; a printout has no tooltips) and the Türkiye average (dashed; its value in the point's tooltip), **placed by their share of the test** (2026-11-19): the axis runs from 0 to the whole test (marked in nets, 0 · 7,5 · 15 · 22,5 · 30 for 30 questions; below 0 only for a negative net), so 2,5 of TYT Din's 5 questions sits where 15 of TYT Matematik's 30 does and every subject's red and green areas are drawn fairly; the numbers written stay nets. Without the counts, nets on their own range. The room between the two lines is **shaded green where the students are above, red where below**, split where the lines cross; no shading next to an exam without a Türkiye average. The students' line is ink rather than the subject's colour so that only the shading is red or green. Mean net = the mean over the students whose net the list prints (a blank is not a 0). Exam names run under the chart (long names wrap, measured in pixels, never cut; with many exams on spaced points counted back from the newest).
4. **Konular**: a row per topic **in the şube's own plan order** (2026-11-18, `planaGore`): the order of the class's Müfredat plan (`D.konuPlani[şube kodu]`, week by week; the class finds its şube code in the timetables, 12/A = 301; the lesson by name, MATEMATİK-A → *Matematik AYT*, TARİH.1-A → *Tarih*; entries tagged (TYT)/(AYT) only for their session; *Genel Tekrar* ignored). Konu Takibi's MEB topic names meet the plan's own names by shared words (each cut to five letters, one a prefix of the other): 60% of the topic's words, or every word of the entry, or half when a shared word is specific (not *anlam, sistem, kavram, problem, fonksiyon, grafik, derece…*). A topic the plan does not name stays right after the topic before it in the kazanım codes. Without a plan: the kazanım codes (`kodSirasi`: E.GEO.9.4.3.1 → grade 9, unit 4, topic 3, kazanım 1), a **coloured square** per exam (columns oldest first by the date Konu Takibi resolves, its own else the school lists', `examDate`; an exam with no date anywhere goes last):
   - **one solid spectrum** (2026-11-19, `spektrum`): deep red → orange → amber → light green → deep green; **red** behind Türkiye on the same questions, **amber** about level, **green** ahead, further out with the gap (the ends at ±20 points); no square is pale or see-through;
   - a gap **within chance** sits nearer the middle (its place × 0,6): z = (d − b) / √(Σ n·p·(1 − p) · (1 + (q − 1)·0,2)) with |z| < 1,96;
   - **under the chart** (2026-11-19): when every exam of the table is on the chart, each exam's squares sit right under its point (the chart's left column is the topic names'), every exam named under the chart; on a narrow screen a topic's name has its own line above its squares;
   - **dashed empty square**: no question on the topic that exam;
   - no number in a square: hovering shows the two percentages (*öğrencileriniz %13, Türkiye %70*), and the folded appendix has every figure;
   - **Bütün konular** (last row): the whole subject on the same questions;
   - **Kazanımlar** (tick box, screen only): shows each topic's kazanımlar as smaller rows under it (number, name, smaller squares); hidden by default and on paper unless ticked.

   Under the table one key: four squares from red to green between *Türkiye’nin gerisinde* and *Türkiye’nin önünde*, and the empty square *soru yok*. Exams with nets but no topic data are named in one quiet line (*Konu verisi henüz yok: …*). Because b uses Türkiye's success on the same questions, a hard question does not paint a topic red on its own.
5. **Hesaplama ayrıntıları ve ham veriler** (folded): the rules with their formulas, and the raw tables: per exam (students, mean net, Türkiye average, gap) and per topic and exam (n, d, b, gap, z). The list's last row, the 1st school and the school's place are still read by `netSerisi` but no longer shown.

## Use and print

- **Öğretmen** filters to one teacher (*Bütün öğretmenler* by default) within the chosen grade.
- **Bu rapor nasıl okunur?** (folded, once, on screen) holds the reading notes. On paper (2026-11-18) a teacher's sheet carries only the report's name and day, the teacher, the blocks and their keys (the chart's legend, the squares' key): no paragraphs of notes, no tick box, no *Konu verisi henüz yok* line; a teacher fits one sheet.
- **PDF indir** (2026-11-18) builds the PDF itself, the way the Öğrenci raporu does (`sayfaSvg` → one picture per page → `pdfYap` with pdf-lib, saved with `indir`): one A4 landscape page per teacher shown (a teacher with no data has no page), so the browser's header, the site's title and address and page numbers never appear. The page has the Öğrenci raporu's look: a warm page (#f6f5f0), white soft-cornered cards (rx 14), the same ink and muted greys, red #bf6951 and green #168d7d. Top: the report's name, the teacher, the lessons and classes, the day. The blocks with a topic table are large cards side by side (up to three a row), each with the two numbers and the pill, the shaded chart and the coloured squares (a wide card puts the chart beside the squares); the blocks with nets only are small cards along the bottom (up to six a row). Cards are as tall as their content; rows and the chart shrink so that the page always holds every block (8 blocks and a 24-topic table fit). The kazanımlar (2026-11-19) go under their topics when every card keeps rows of at least 11 px; otherwise the teacher's page is followed by *Kazanımlar* pages (`kazanimSayfalari`): each lesson's topics with their kazanımlar under them, the same squares, in two white columns a page. A wide, low small card puts the numbers beside the chart. One key at the foot. The students' line is the accent blue (#2c6aa8 on paper, `--vurgu` on screen). On the student pages the same, in the student's words. Ctrl+P still prints the page itself with the print styles.
- Nothing is stored and nothing is sent; the page reads the notebook only.

### The paper (A4 landscape)

- **Each teacher's sheets stand alone.** A teacher starts a new page under a letterhead: *Öğretmen raporu* (with the grade when one is chosen) and the day it was printed, the teacher's name, the lessons and classes, and beside them **Bu raporu okurken**: *Kimleri kapsıyor?* (the classes the teacher teaches, as one group; no student, class or teacher comparison), *Veriler nereden geliyor?* (the school's exam result lists; the Türkiye average from the publisher's Türkiye geneli results for the same exams; nothing estimated), *Nasıl okunur?* (+ and green above the Türkiye average, − and red below; net gaps in net, topic gaps in percentage points).
- **A page per block.** The teacher's first block follows the letterhead; every further block starts its own page with the teacher's name beside its title.
- **Nothing is cut.** The takeaway, the boxes and the chart stay together; the topic table stays together (it usually takes the next sheet, with the block's and the teacher's name beside its heading, so a loose sheet says whose it is); a table too long for one page repeats its header and never splits a row. There is no empty or nearly empty page.
- **The table fits the page** whatever the number of exams: the name and the two summary columns keep their width, the exams share the rest; above twelve exams the names stand upright.
- **Its own palette and type**: dark ink on white, Arial, the subjects' colours fixed. The screen theme does not reach it, a dark one included, and every fill prints even with "background graphics" off.
- The page title, the toolbar, the screen's folded notes and a closed appendix are not printed. The charts are drawn again at the paper's width, so their values stay readable.

## Speed

The first version froze the browser on a real school: every block rebuilt Öğrenci raporları' student base, and that base copied every school list for every student and compared every etüt row with every student. Now:

- one page reads the school lists and Konu Takibi once and works out each exam's topic comparison once for all students; every block takes its classes' students from them;
- the page no longer builds the student base at all (it was needed only for the etüts);
- the charts read all their widths before drawing any (one layout instead of one per chart).

Measured while the page still read the etüts: on a synthetic school of 500 students, 14 weeks of lists and two etüts per student a week, it opened in under a second in Chromium. It now does less work than that (not measured again).

## Türkiye ortalaması karşılaştırması (one student)

**Denemeler ▾ → Türkiye karşılaştırması (öğrenci)** (rehber öğretmen, `ogrencitr`, `OGRETMEN_RAPOR.gorunumOgrenci`) is the same report for one student, to hand to the student or their family: **Sınıf düzeyi → Şube → Öğrenci** (or every student of the şube, each on their own sheets). The student is found by the school number in the lists and in Konu Takibi (`netSerisi` / `konuIzgarasi` with `nolar`).

- A block per exam subject the student has nets or topic answers in, TYT before AYT (`ogrenciModeli`, `TUM_KODLAR`), with the same takeaway, boxes, chart, topic table with kazanımlar, and appendix.
- **Exams with nets but no topic data are named under the topic table (2026-11-18)**: “Konu tablosunda olmayan denemeler: …”. On Öğretmen raporu it names the file to add: the publisher's Kazanım Karnesi of that session (each student's kazanım results). The Sınav özeti, Madde Analizi and Performans analizi are question totals for the whole school, with no student or class results, so they cannot fill a column; Konu Takibi → Kaynaklar says per exam which session's karnesi is missing. On a student's sheet the note says only that those exams have no topic results yet. A missing column is otherwise read as “no question”.
- **AYT tests the student did not take are left out (2026-11-18).** Every student gets the whole AYT booklet but answers only their field's tests (SAY: Matematik + Fen; EA: TDE-SB1 + Matematik; SÖZ: TDE-SB1 + SB2). A test left wholly blank used to show as a 0 net under the Türkiye average and as red topics (an SAY student's Edebiyat, Tarih-1, Coğrafya-1; an EA student's Fizik, Kimya, Biyoloji). Now a subject's net counts only when its test was answered (`SIRALAMA.denedi`: any D or Y in the list), and a wholly blank test gives no topic gaps (Konu Takibi, below). The same applies to the teacher's report and to the student's own Türkiye karşılaştırması.
- The words are the student's (`YAZI.ogrenci`): *Netin*, *Türkiye ortalamasının altındasın; önceki denemeye göre aradaki fark azaldı*, *Konular: Türkiye’ye göre neredesin?*, a reading note *Kimi kapsıyor? Yalnızca seni…*. No other student's name, no ranking.
- The school's place and the İlk 100 yardstick are about schools, so they are not on a student's report.
- With one student a topic has only a few questions an exam, so most cells sit near the middle of the spectrum (within chance); the appendix says to read the direction from the deep cells and from a colour that repeats.

### On the student's own device

YKS students have their own tab, **Türkiye karşılaştırması** (`kiyas`, `OGRETMEN_RAPOR.gorunumKendi`), with the same page for themselves and its own Yazdır / PDF. Their notebook has no school lists and no Konu Takibi, so the teacher's app works the comparison out and sends it in the student's package (`ogrenciPaketi` → `turkiyeKiyas`, built by `kiyasOzeti`; the student's notebook keeps it as `D.turkiyeKiyas`). It is compact: per block the exams `[ad, g, net, Türkiye]` and per topic and kazanım the cells `[d, b, n]`; `kiyasAc` builds the same blocks back (the chance test uses the one student's variance). It stays under 250 000 characters: the kazanımlar are left out first, then the topic tables. It is as new as the teacher's last publish; the page says from when.
