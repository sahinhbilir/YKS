# Etüt etkisi (guidance teacher)

The **Etüt etkisi** page asks one question of the school's own records: *do students gain more between two exams when they took etüts in between?* It does not compare average nets and it does not sort students into groups by their etüt counts. It looks at **how much each student's result moved**, measured against the Türkiye average, and compares every student **with themself**. It sits in the teacher menu under **Öğrenciler**, after Etütler, and shares the Etütler look (`src/etutler.css`, root `#eeKok`).

## Where the data comes from

Nothing is added, stored or uploaded. The page reads the analysis base of **Öğrenci raporları** (`OGRENCI_RAPOR.temelOgrenciler()`): the merged students with their exam results (the net of every lesson) and their etüt sessions (date, lesson, teacher, from Etütler). It also reads the lists of **Türkiye geneli** (`TURKIYE.oku()`, `TURKIYE.ortalamalar`, `TURKIYE.model`) for the Türkiye average net of every lesson in every exam. The page never writes to the notebook and never reaches a student packet; the choices live in `EK.ee*` only (`eeDuzey`, `eeOturum`, `eeDers`, `eeDonem`, `eeHoca`).

## How it is computed

Per lesson and session (**Sınıf**, **Oturum**, **Ders**, **Dönem** as before: *Tümü* or *Son 4 deneme*; a lesson's etüt course is turned into a lesson the way Etütler does it, and an etüt counts for both sessions whatever “TYT” or “AYT” its course name carries, because the school writes “MATEMATİK TYT” for AYT etüts too and one etüt often covers both). Exam days are never etüt days.

**Türkiye farkı.** d = the student's net − the Türkiye average net of that lesson in that exam, so how hard the exam was drops out. The exam is matched to its Türkiye list by name, the way Türkiye geneli matches it (`TURKIYE.ortalamalar`). AYT Coğrafya and Tarih are two parts for the student and in the list; both sides are summed. An exam without a list for that lesson is not used. If no exam of the period has a list, the raw net is used and a banner says so: *Türkiye geneli listesi yok: ham net artışı kullanıldı; deneme zorluğu ayıklanmadı. Türkiye geneli sayfasından ekleyin.*

### The main measure: change since the etüt, by etüt count (`toplamDegisim`, `regres`)

1. **Windows.** Every student with two exams that count. A student with etüts: from the last exam strictly before their first etüt of the lesson (the first exam of the period when the etüt came earlier, marked *etüt bu denemeden önce başladı*) to their last exam. A student with no etüt: first exam to last. **Toplam değişim** = d at the end − d at the start; small swings in between do not cancel (+5 after the etüt, then −1 is +4). An etüt with no exam after it leaves the student out (*etütten sonra deneme yok*).
2. **The model.** One regression over every student: change = a_pair + b·d_start + γ·e. a_pair is an intercept for the (start exam, end exam) pair and absorbs the school's trend of that pair; b·d_start is the rebound control (a student who starts low rises anyway); e is the number of etüts of the lesson in the student's window. Each pair is demeaned within itself, so students with fewer etüts are the comparison for those with more and nobody has to be without etüt. A pair with fewer than 3 students borrows: a = (n/3)·own + (1 − n/3)·pooled intercept (a pair with one student uses a third of its own). A column with no variation inside the pairs gets no coefficient. **Beklenen, etütsüz** = a_pair + b·d_start (the student with zero etüts); **Etüt sonrası fark** = change − Beklenen.
3. **Headline.** γ with a 95% interval: *Etüt sayısı arttıkça artış: her etüt ≈ +0,4 net (olası aralık …)*, with how much the etüt students changed and what they would have been expected to. The interval is the 2.5% and 97.5% percentiles of a cluster bootstrap over students (1000 resamples, fixed seed `TOHUM`, same data give the same interval), and the verdict (`kararToplam`) follows it: *Etüt sayısı arttıkça artış belirgin…*, *Belirgin bir fark yok.*, *Etüt sayısı arttıkça artış daha az…*, or *Yeterli veri yok* when the etüt counts barely vary (e.g. everyone has the same count), fewer than 8 students identify γ, or the interval is wider than 3 net per etüt.
4. **Teachers.** The same model with one γ per teacher (e counted for that teacher only; the other teachers' etüts are held constant) gives each teacher's own gain per etüt, **Her etüt için artış**, with its interval, the students and etüts they had, and **Toplam katkı** = γ × the etüts that teacher gave (their own pocket). No share is split: a student with two teachers is in both rows with their own counts. Ranked by Toplam katkı, ties share a rank (1, 1, 3); under 5 students no rank (—), drawn faint, last. The name filter limits the student list and the dots to that teacher's etüts.
5. **Outliers (modified z-score, Iglewicz & Hoaglin: M = 0,6745·(x − median)/MAD, flagged above 3,5).** They are never dropped silently: listed in the collapsed **Hesaba katılmayan uç sonuçlar (n)** with the reason, marked in the students table, hollow in the charts.
   - *One exam that does not fit a student's own record* (a blank test, an illness): each result's deviation from the student's own median d (students with three results or more), against the MAD pooled over all students of the lesson (each has few exams), with a floor of 2 net so a pool of identical values flags nothing. A flagged result is not a window's start or end (the nearest unflagged exam is used), is left out of the small line and is drawn as a hollow dot. Several results per student and many students are handled the same way.
   - *A student whose change is extreme:* fit, flag every student whose residual has |M| > 3,5 (median and MAD of the residuals of the students kept, floor 1 net), refit without them, then judge **all** students again against the new fit (a student flagged earlier is let back in if they fit the cleaner model), until the set stops changing (at most 5 rounds), so several extreme students cannot hide each other. At most 20% of the students are put aside; if more would be flagged, only the most extreme fifth is, with the line *uç sonuç çok fazla: en uç 20% dışarıda*. Flagged students are out of the fitted line, the headline, the teachers' γ and Toplam katkı, and the bootstrap resamples the rest.

### Supporting view: between exams (`etkiHesapla`)

Collapsed under **Ayrıntı: deneme aralarına göre**. For every pair of consecutive exams a student sat: g = d₂ − d₁ and e = the etüts strictly between the two exam days. Within-student fixed effects g = aᵢ + b·e + c·(d₁ − d̄ᵢ) + ε (the c term controls the rebound after a bad exam), fitted by demeaning within the student; only students whose etüt count changes between their intervals identify b. 95% interval by cluster bootstrap over students, a sentence on it, the paired comparison (students with both kinds of intervals) and the bars by etüts in the interval (0, 1, 2, 3, 4+; faint under 5 intervals). The two methods never share the headline.

**Students left out**, listed apart with the reason: *yalnızca 1 denemeye girdi*, *bu dönemde denemeye girmedi*, *bu dersin neti yok*, *Türkiye listesi olan iki deneme yok*, *etütten sonra deneme yok*. **Coverage:** weekdays in the period that no added etüt report covers are counted; the page warns that etüt counts may be incomplete.

## What the page shows

Top to bottom, in plain Turkish with every number labelled:

1. Context line, the **headline** (`#eeOzet`), the **verdict** (`#eeKarar`), the counts of students (with etüts / without, left out) and the one-line limit.
2. Controls (Sınıf, Oturum, Ders, Dönem), then the banners (matching warnings, the missing-list banner or the exams without a list, coverage).
3. **Etüt aldıkça artış** (`#eeFark`): a dot per student, x = the etüts they took (the chosen teacher's when one is chosen), y = their change after the start level and the school's trend are taken out (Etüt sonrası fark), the fitted line with its slope written on it ("her etüt +0,4 net"), the zero line, no names; extreme students are hollow and labelled *aykırı, hesaba katılmadı*. Chosen over per-student bars because it shows the question itself: more etüts, more gain, and who the line stands on.
4. **Okulun Türkiye'deki yeri** (`#eeGrafik`): the lesson's line from Türkiye geneli for that grade and session (`TURKIYE.model`, with the list kind and school chosen on the Türkiye geneli page, and every deneme of the grade: none is cut by the students' period, since a deneme without a Sıralamalar list is dated by its report's print day; the line under the chart names the list, session, lesson and denemeler drawn) as the school's yüzdelik dilim ("ilk %12", higher is better), with the weekly etüt count of the lesson beneath on the same dates. The dilim helpers are TURKIYE's own (`dilimNokta`, `dilimdenT`, `dilimYazi`, `dilimKisa`, `DILIM_TIKLARI`); if missing, the page uses dilim = 100·(1−Φ((T−50)/10)) with the same wording. Without a list for the grade the panel says so with a button to Türkiye geneli.
5. **Öğretmenlere göre** (`#eeHocalar`): rank, teacher, students, etüts, Toplam katkı, Her etüt için artış with its interval under it.
6. **Öğrenciler** (`#eeOgrenciler`): Etütten önce (exam and d), Son deneme, Toplam değişim, Beklenen (etütsüz), Etüt sonrası fark (▲ green / ▼ red), Etüt, the teachers with their etüt counts ("Sentetik Öğretmen A · 6 etüt"), and a small line of d with a dotted marker at the first etüt; rows of extreme students are faint and marked. Then the collapsed list of students left out (`#eeDisarida`) and of extreme results (`#eeUc`).
7. Collapsed **Ayrıntı: deneme aralarına göre** (`#eeAralik`) and **Nasıl hesaplanır** (`#eeNasil`, plain words).

Numeric headers and numbers share the class `sayi` (right-aligned); on a phone the non-essential columns are hidden so nothing scrolls sideways. Charts are drawn at the box's real width (the interval bars are drawn when their block is opened).

## What it cannot tell

It shows an **association, not a cause.** A student may go to etüt because of something that also changes their results (a new study plan, a change at home). The rebound control removes the most common trap, not every one. Few students or few intervals give wide intervals, which is why small bars and teachers are drawn faint and why the verdict can be *Yeterli veri yok*.

## Code and tests

- `src/etutetkisi.js` and `src/etutetkisi.css` are embedded into `index.html` by `node scripts/embed-etutetkisi.cjs` (`--check` only verifies). The pure model (`model`, `beklenenModel`, `kararToplam`, `etkiHesapla`, `bootstrap`, `karar`, `eslestirme`, `kovalar`, `trOrtalama`, `durus`) is exported with the page. The page is `gorunumEtutEtki()` under the tab `etutetki` (rehber only).
- `test/etutetkisi-regression.cjs` checks d with Türkiye averages (the AYT two-part sum, the name match through the real `TURKIYE.ortalamalar`); the teacher's example (+5 then −1 is +4); a planted +0,4 per etüt recovered when every student took etüt; two teachers with planted gains +0,6 and 0 told apart with no split, and Toplam katkı = γ × etüts; the rebound and a school-wide trend giving γ = 0; a first etüt before the first exam and an etüt with no later exam; no variation giving *Yeterli veri yok*; headline text, verdict sentences and bootstrap determinism; teacher ranks and thresholds; outliers (a −15 net blank test changes neither window nor γ, three extreme students flagged, two pulling the same way both caught, the 20% cap and its message, the MAD floor, no false flags on clean fixtures); the interval method (hand-worked fixture, planted +0,5 per etüt against a dummy-variable fit, rebound control, paired comparison, bars); the missing-list fallback; left-out reasons; periods, coverage, the page (sections in order, no tier labels, collapsed blocks), the charts and dilim helpers, states, menu and privacy.
- `test/tutarlilik-regression.cjs` runs one synthetic fixture through Deneme panosu, Etüt etkisi, Öğrenci raporları, Yoklama and Etütler.
- `test/etutetkisi-browser.cjs` runs on desktop (light) and at 390 px (dark): the menu, the sections in order, the charts at their real width in real-pixel type, the collapsed interval block opening and drawing, every numeric header over its numbers, the teacher filter, Dönem, Ders and Sınıf, the left-out list, the missing-list banner, redraw on resize, no sideways scrolling.
- The synthetic students and Türkiye lists (no real data) are built inside the tests.
