# Türkiye geneli (guidance teacher)

The **Türkiye geneli** page charts how much the school's nets rise from exam to exam compared with the publisher's schools across Türkiye, and where it stands among them. It reads the report **Ders Ortalamaları İlk 100 Kurum (Kolej)**: for every subject, the 100 schools with the highest average net and the Türkiye average (*Genel Net*). Each PDF added is one exam, so each new exam adds a point to every line. The page sits in the teacher menu under **Denemeler**, after Sıralamalar, and shares the Etütler look (`src/etutler.css`, root `#tgKok`).

## Getting the report

In Myexamy: select the exam on **Sınavlar**, then **Raporlar → Türkiye Geneli Analiz ve Raporları**, and download the PDF of **“<exam> Ders Ortalamaları İlk 100 Kurum (Kolej)”**. On the page, **PDF ekle** adds one or more of these files (up to 10 at a time). The PDF is read in the browser and is never uploaded.

## Reading the PDF

- Each subject starts on a new page under a title such as `27-YKS-1202, TÜRKÇE-T Dersi Net Sıralı Liste _40 Soru_ (İlk 100 Okul)`. The title gives the exam, the subject and its question count. The columns are Sıralama · Okul Adı · Okul Net · Okul Başarı · Genel Net, and the rows carry on to the next page under the headers only.
- **Ek Felsefe** (`S.FELSEFE-T`, `S.FELSEFE-A`) is left out: it is not read, not kept, and lists stored before lose it the next time the data is saved.
- The page keeps the place, the school and its net. *Başarı* is net ÷ questions, so it is computed again rather than stored. The Türkiye average is the value printed on most rows. The print date comes from the footer.
- A school name split across two lines is joined to its row. An unreadable row is skipped and counted in the status line.
- A PDF with no such title is refused, and so are Şube and Öğrenci lists and a file that mixes two exams.
- **List kind.** The same report set also contains “İlk 100 Kurum” (schools and courses together) and “(Kurs)”. The kind comes from the file name when it says *(Kolej)* or *(Kurs)*. Otherwise it is inferred from the names: no course means Kolej, only courses means Kurs, and a mix means *Tüm kurumlar*. Different kinds are never mixed. When more than one kind is stored, a **Liste** switch appears and Kolej is the default.
- Adding an exam's list again (same exam name and kind) replaces it.

## The exams and the school

- **Grade.** An exam's grade is, in order: the teacher's choice in the Denemeler table's **Sınıf** column; the exam's name (`27-YKS-1202` and `12.SINIF_HAZIR_BULUNUŞLUK_SINAVI` are 12. sınıf, `27-TYT-901` is 9. sınıf); the grade most of its students are in on the same exam's Sıralamalar list. An exam whose grade is still unknown (such as `27-TYT_SIFIR_POZİTİF`) joins the main group, so it is never hidden. The main group is 12. sınıf, otherwise the grade with the most exams. A **Sınıf** switch appears when more than one grade is stored; **Tümü** shows every exam of the list together. Choosing *Otomatik* in the Sınıf column clears the teacher's choice, and adding the same list again keeps it.
- **Lists not in the chart.** When some stored lists are of another kind or grade, a line under the toolbar names them (“Grafikte 3 deneme var. Diğerleri: …”), and each name switches the page to that list.
- **Order and date.** Exams are ordered by date. The date comes from the same exam's school list in Sıralamalar (its corrected date first). Without one, the report's print date is used, and the exam table marks it “rapor”.
- **The school.** **Okul** chooses the school the page follows, and the choice is saved with the lists. Before the teacher chooses, the school named on the Sıralamalar lists is used when exactly one school in the report matches it. Otherwise the page asks the teacher to pick one. A viewing teacher's choice is kept for the visit only.
- **The two exams compared.** In **Artış** they are **İlk deneme → Son deneme**: the first exam of the chart and the latest by default, so the page reads the whole run of mock exams, not one pair. In **Sıra** they are **Önceki → Şimdiki**: the latest and the exam just before it. The first only offers exams before the second. Adding a PDF switches the page to that exam.

## Dilim ve sıra (the default view)

**The headline is the yüzdelik dilim**, the ÖSYM / YKS language teachers already use: “Türkiye'de **ilk %12**”, smaller is better. It is the same calculation as T said in words, nothing is fitted again: **dilim = 100 · (1 − Φ(z))**, z = (T − 50) / 10, from the model's own fit (so it agrees with the estimated place, sıra ≈ 1 + (N − 1)(1 − Φ(z))). For a **complete list**, where places are exact, dilim = sıra ÷ N × 100, so the dilim and the place beside it never disagree. Format: a whole number from 10 up (“ilk %12”), one decimal below 10 (“ilk %8,4”), and never “ilk %0” (the floor is “ilk %0,1”). Under each total a plain band says it in words, on the cut points of T 70 / 60 / 55 / 45 / 40: dilim ≤ 2,3 “Türkiye’nin en üst okulları arasında”; ≤ 15,9 “Ortalamanın belirgin üstünde”; ≤ 30,9 “Ortalamanın üstünde”; < 69,1 “Türkiye ortalaması civarında”; < 84,1 “Ortalamanın altında”; otherwise “Ortalamanın belirgin altında”. The place keeps its form, “≈ 183 okul içinde 23.” (“≈” before the number of schools when N is estimated, before the place when the place is). **T stays only in the hover tooltips (“T 61,6 · net 78,4 · σ …”) and in the “Kaynaklar ve hesaplama” note at the bottom of the page**, which also says: “Dilim, okulun Türkiye’deki okulların yüzde kaçının gerisinde kaldığını gösterir: ilk %12 = okulların %88’inden iyi.”

**Change pills** read “▲ ilk %31 → ilk %12”; the colour and the arrow are still decided by ΔT (green ▲ when T rose by more than 0,5, red ▼ when it fell by more than 0,5, grey within ±0,5). Where a pill is small (subject cards) it reads “▲ %31 → %12” and the note above the cards carries “ilk %” and “▲ yeşil: üst dilime çıktı (iyileşti) · ▼ kırmızı: alt dilime düştü”.

**Görünüm: Dilim ve sıra** is the default; Artış and Sıra stay as the other two views. It is the school-level twin of Deneme panosu (`src/denemepano.js`), with schools in place of students and the followed school highlighted: it answers "how far above the average of Türkiye's schools is this school, how does it rank, and how much did it gain against the other schools?". The KPI strip and its tiles use Deneme panosu's classes (`dp-kpi`).

- **Karşılaştır** (range chips): **Tüm dönem**, **Son deneme**, **Son 3 deneme**, **Son 5 deneme** and **Seç**. A range is first → latest exam (Son 3 deneme is three exams, two steps); **Seç** shows the two exam pickers. Chips without enough exams are disabled. Every ΔT value and every increase place below reads this range.
- **KPI strip:** the primary tile is the school's change over the range, “ilk %31 → ilk %12”, with a coloured words pill (“▲ üst dilime çıktı”) and its increase place ("Artış sırası 12. / 150 okul"; "≈" when the school had to be placed from its own net, see below). The supporting tiles are the current dilim (“ilk %12”) with its band, its place (printed, or "≈") and the change since the first exam (“▲ ilk %31 → ilk %12”), **Ortalamanın üstünde** (subjects in the first 50 %, with the first exam's count) and **Okul sayısı** (N; "tam liste" or the estimate).
- **Grafik: Dilim | Sıra | Artış sırası** (one chart at a time, Dilim by default). Every subject is a thin line in the colour of its card and the chosen total is the bold line; the line under the pointer takes the stage and the tooltip lists the total and every subject, sorted. A subject card or table row, or a click on a line, shows that subject alone (with the dashed 50 line in T); clicking it again or **Tüm dersler** returns to all lines. A subject with no value in an exam leaves a gap.
  - **Dilim:** the y axis is inverted like Sıra: “ilk %1” at the top, %50 in the middle, down to %95. It is drawn on the **T (z) scale** and only labelled with dilim values (ilk %1, %5, %15, %30, %50, %70, %85, %95): a linear dilim axis would squash the top schools into one corner. Axis title “Türkiye yüzdelik dilimi (küçük = daha iyi)”; the dashed line is %50, “Türkiye’nin ortasındaki okul”; the end label is the latest dilim.
  - **Sıra:** 1 at the top, down to about N. A printed place is a filled marker on a solid line, an estimate (the school is outside the list) a hollow marker on a dashed one. A total has no ranking in the report, so its place is an estimate (exact for complete lists).
  - **Artış sırası:** the x axis is the transitions of the range (1201→1202, …); the y axis is the school's place by ΔT in each transition, 1 at the top.
- **Subject cards** show the dilim, the place and the change pill, with the mini line of the chosen measure only.
- **Artış sıralaması:** the first three schools and the three on each side of the followed school, **ranked by ΔT** (fair to schools already near the top, where one step is harder) but **shown as “ilk %31 → ilk %12”**. A line under the list says: “Sıra, okulun kendi düzeyine göre ne kadar ilerlediğine göredir; üst dilimlerde ilerlemek daha zordur.”
- **Okullar table** (twin of the student table): the schools in the lists of the range for the chosen measure (a subject or the total) with Dilim (latest, smallest first by default when sorted), Sıra (latest), Değişim (range, shown as “ilk %31 → ilk %12”, sorted by ΔT), Artış sırası and Net (latest). It sorts by any column (default Değişim, largest rise first; a second click reverses), schools missing from one of the exams show "—" for ΔT, the followed school is highlighted and pinned at the bottom of the table box, and **Bizim okul** jumps to its row. The table shows the publisher's public school-level figures already stored by the page; no student data.
- **Hover:** the tooltip carries the numbers: the dilim, the place, “T 61,6 · net 78,4 · σ 5,12”, "N ≈ 180 okul (derslerde 160–220)" (the pooled N with the spread of the subjects' own fits), or "tam liste · 80 okul". "≈" and N are not repeated in the page text.
- **A gap, not a guess:** T and the estimate need the school's own net. In the list it is the row's net. Outside it, the report prints nothing, so the page uses **Sıralamalar' school average (Kurum Ortalamaları) of the same exam and subject**, only when Sıralamalar names this school and the net is there (the tooltip says "Sıralamalar kurum ortalaması"). Otherwise the point is empty and the tooltip says why.
- **No total:** a session whose total cannot be built (AYT without the subjects of a field) shows **Ders ortalaması T**, the mean of the subjects' T, labelled so, and no place.

### Artış sırası (ΔT)

ΔT = T(later) − T(earlier) for a subject or the total, per school, ranked among the schools that have the measure in **both** exams (for complete lists every school; for truncated lists the schools in both top lists; a total needs the school in every list of the total). **T, not raw nets, is used** because T is standardised within each exam, so increases stay comparable when exams differ in difficulty and spread. ΔT is rounded to 0.01 and equal increases share a place. The followed school, when it is missing from a list in one of the exams, is placed among the ranked schools by ΔT from its own T (its own net, see "A gap, not a guess"); its place is marked "≈" and the tooltip shows "Ortak okul" (how many schools were ranked). The Artış view (net based) stays as it was.

### Complete lists

A list with fewer rows than its nominal 100 is the whole population of schools, not a truncated top (the teacher's exam with 80 schools). For such a subject N is the row count, μ and σ are the **empirical mean and SD (over the schools)** of the listed nets, T = 50 + 10·(x − μ)/σ is exact and the place is the printed one, never estimated. The exam's N is the row count when all its lists are complete; a mixed exam is handled per subject (a complete subject keeps its own N, the truncated ones are pooled among themselves). For a total over complete lists the school's nets are summed per school (matched by name; every school must be in every list) and the σ and the exact place come from those sums, with no correlation correction; otherwise the Case II path below is used. Note the basis: a complete list's μ is the **school-level** mean, while the truncated case uses Genel Net (student-level), which is why the two can differ slightly (the tooltip says "tam liste").

### Method

1. **Model.** For one exam, list kind and subject the list gives the top schools' nets x_k by place k and the Türkiye average μ. The schools' mean nets are taken as Normal(μ, σ) over N schools, so x_k ≈ μ + σ·Φ⁻¹(1 − (k − 0.5)/N).
2. **Fit.** For every N from (last printed place + 20) up to 6 000 (step 10 up to 1 000, then step 50, then step 1 around the best) σ = Σ(x − μ)q / Σq² with q = Φ⁻¹(1 − (k − 0.5)/N); the N with the least squared error wins. The fit reports σ, N and RMSE (in nets). Printed places are used, so ties keep their place.
3. **Pooled N** per exam and list kind: the median of the subjects' N over the subjects with RMSE / σ < 0.35 (Ek Felsefe is not stored). Each subject's σ is then fitted again with that N fixed. If no subject fits, there is no N and no T for that exam. The spread of the per-subject N is shown in the tooltip.
4. **T** = 50 + 10·(x_s − μ)/σ. **Place** outside the list = 1 + (N − 1)(1 − Φ((x_s − μ)/σ)), rounded, and never above the list's last printed place + 1.
5. **Total.** The report has only per-subject lists, so there is no total ranking to fit. μ_total = Σμ_d and x_total = Σ x_d (every subject needed). σ_total² = Σσ_i² + 2·Σ_{i<j} R_ij σ_i σ_j, where R_ij is the between-subject correlation of school means, **corrected for range restriction**:
   - **Why:** the lists hold only the top 100 schools, the upper tail, and correlations measured there are attenuated (on the real 27-YKS-1202 list the median observed r among TYT subjects was 0.66, SÖZ 0.34).
   - **r_ij:** the correlation of the nets of the schools listed in both subjects (at least 30 schools; fewer is "too few").
   - **Correction:** Thorndike (1949) Case II, R = r·u / √(1 − r² + r²u²), with u = σ_fit / s_obs, the fitted population σ over the SD of the subject's listed nets, averaged over the two subjects; R is clamped to [0, 0.98].
   - **Fallbacks:** a pair with too few common schools takes the median R of the other pairs in the same total. If there is none, R = 0.8 and the tooltip says "varsayılan korelasyon". Otherwise it shows the median R used: "derslerarası r ≈ 0,86 (düzeltilmiş)".
   - **Second reading:** the tooltip of a total also shows **Ders ortalaması T**, the plain mean of the subjects' T.
   - **Assumptions:** the relation between subjects is linear and homoscedastic, and selection works on the measured nets themselves. Case II is exact for selection on one variable. Here every subject lists its own top 100, so the common schools are selected on both; with u taken from each subject's whole list the correction is approximate and tends to under-correct (in a simulation of 180 schools with ρ = 0.85 it gave 0.83 from an observed 0.72; with a smaller share listed it under-corrects more). A smaller R makes σ_total smaller and the total's T more extreme.

### Limits

- σ is the spread **between schools' means**, not between students. A school's net here is an average, so T = 60 means "one σ of school means above the average school", not a student-level T.
- The method assumes normally distributed school means over N schools; the top 100 only constrain the upper tail. N is an estimate (real file: 153–221 across subjects) and so is every place beyond the list. Subjects with few questions (5-question lessons) have coarse nets and fit less tightly.
- Sıralamalar' *Kurum Ortalamaları* may cover a different group of students than the publisher's school mean (for example all grades or a different campus grouping); T outside the list is only as good as that match.
- A total's σ depends on the corrected correlations (above); the total's place is an estimate in every exam. The label "Türkiye ortalaması (okullar)" sits at the right end above the 50 line.

## Artış

Exams differ in difficulty, so an increase is read against the Türkiye average's increase between the same exams: **Türkiye’ye göre** = the school's change − the Türkiye average's change, in nets. By default the page covers the whole run, first exam → latest: the **total** is the last exam against the first, and the **average per exam** is the total over the steps between neighbouring exams (a run of 4 exams has 3 steps).

- **Totals:** the sum of the subjects' average nets. TYT takes every TYT subject (120 questions without Ek Felsefe). AYT has one total per field, chosen with **Toplam**: SAY (Matematik, Geometri, Fizik, Kimya, Biyoloji), EA (Edebiyat, Tarih-1, Coğrafya-1, Matematik, Geometri) and SÖZ (Edebiyat, Tarih-1, Coğrafya-1, Tarih-2, Coğrafya-2, Felsefe Grubu, Din Kültürü). A total is missing in an exam where any of its subjects is missing for that school.
- **Sentence and cards:** for the total (or the chosen subject), the change in net (total over the run and **Deneme başına ortalama**, with the Türkiye average's), then the increase against Türkiye, the **Artış sırası**, the school's own change with both nets and the Türkiye average's change, how many subjects rose more than Türkiye, and the subjects that rose the most and fell furthest behind (by success points, so subjects of different lengths compare).
- **Net** chart: the total's net (or the chosen subject's) exam by exam, the school against the Türkiye average (dashed). A total missing in an exam is bridged by a dashed line.
- **Denemeler arası net değişimi:** a table with a row for the total and for every subject and **Toplam** (the total of the net changes, the first exam against the last) and **Ortalama** (per exam, the total over the steps), then a column for every exam after the first: the school's change in net from the exam before, and last the Türkiye average's total and average. A step is empty when the school is outside a list in one of its exams (the net is not printed); the total and the average still come from the first and last exams. A subject's row opens that subject.
- **Artış sıralaması:** every school that has the measure in both the first and the last exam, by its increase (equal increases share a place): the first three and the three on each side of the school, with the Türkiye average's increase where it falls.
- **Subject cards:** the increase against Türkiye, the school's and the Türkiye average's changes and the increase ranking place; the mini line is the net minus the Türkiye average, so its slope is the increase against Türkiye.
- **Türkiye’ye göre artış:** a bar per subject, in success points.
- **Dersler** table: İlk and Son nets, Toplam değişim, the Türkiye average's change, Türkiye’ye göre, Türkiye’ye göre / deneme (the average per exam against Türkiye), Artış sırası (out of how many schools) and the current place.
- With one exam, the page asks for a second one.

## Sıra

**Görünüm: Sıra** shows where the school stands.

- **Sentence and cards:** the exam, the list kind and the school. The cards show the average place across the session's subjects, how many subjects the school is in the top 100 for, how many are above the Türkiye average, the best place, and the subjects that rose and fell the most since the previous exam. A school outside the list counts as place N + 1 (101) in the average.
- **Sıra** (main chart): place 1 is at the top. With no subject chosen, every subject of the session (TYT or AYT) is a line and the bold line is the average place. The line under the pointer is highlighted and the others fade; ↑/↓ moves between lines and a click or Enter opens that subject. A subject outside the top 100 sits on the dotted **İlk 100 dışı** lane, joined to its other points by dashed lines. The previous and current exams' columns are shaded. Hovering or ←/→ shows an exam's figures, and Home/End jump to the first and last exam.
- **Subject cards:** place, places moved (▲/▼), net and Türkiye average, plus a small chart of the place where higher is better. A card opens its subject; clicking it again, or **Tüm dersler**, goes back.
- **Türkiye ortalamasına göre** (no subject chosen): for every subject in the current exam, the school's success minus the Türkiye average's, in points. Bars to the right are above the average and bars to the left are below it.
- **A chosen subject** adds two parts:
  - **Net:** the school's net, the Türkiye average (dashed) and the band from the list's last school to its first. Outside the list the school's net is not printed, so the point is a hollow dot on the band's lower edge.
  - **Sıralamada yakın:** the first school and the four schools on each side of the followed one, with net differences and the Türkiye average placed where it falls. It also shows the place one more net would give, and the gap to the school just above.
- **Dersler** table (Sıra): place and change, previous place, net, success, Türkiye average, difference, the place with one more net, and the first school.

## On both views

- **Denemeler** table: every stored list (all grades and kinds) with its date, its number of subjects and how many of them hold the school. **Sınıf** sets a list's grade, **Sil** deletes one list and **Tüm listeleri sil** deletes them all. A viewing teacher sees the page but cannot add lists, delete them or save the school.
- **Çizgi: Köşeli / Yumuşak** shapes the lines, as on the other boards (docs/grafikler.md).

## Storage and privacy

- The data is kept in the teacher notebook as `D.turkiye = {surum:1, ts, okul, adlar:[…], sinavlar:[{ad, liste, yaz, ts, duzey?, dersler:[[kod, soru, genel, satırlar]]}]}`; `duzey` is present only when the teacher set the grade (0 for Diğer). `adlar` is the school-name dictionary and `genel` is the Türkiye average in hundredths. `satırlar` is the list in printed order as `name:net` pairs in base 36 (the name's index in `adlar`, the net in hundredths), with a third part only when the printed place differs from the row's position. `yaz` is the print day.
- One exam's Kolej list takes about 15 KB. Limits: 40 lists, 250 KB in total, and no addition that would push the notebook over the whole-notebook limit (`DEFTER_HAM_UST_SINIR`, 2 500 000 bytes of JSON; the cloud copy is compressed, see docs/cloud-notebook-recovery.md). A failed save leaves the data unchanged. `dogrula` checks the structure on every read and write; unreadable data is shown as a warning and never overwritten.
- The data travels with the teacher's cloud backup. Student packets are built from a field whitelist and never carry it, and students never see the page.

## Code and tests

- `src/turkiye.js` and `src/turkiye.css` are embedded into `index.html` by `node scripts/embed-turkiye.cjs` (`--check` only verifies). The PDF text is read with `denemePdfSayfalari` from Denemeler, and lines are drawn with `GRAFIK_CIZGI` (`src/grafik.js`).
- `test/turkiye-regression.cjs` covers reading (pages, two-line names, skipped rows, list kinds, refused reports), storage (encoding, replacing, printed places, damaged data, limits), Ek Felsefe left out, exams shown together (grade from the name, Sıralamalar or the teacher; grade-less exams in the main group; the note on other lists), the model (order and Sıralamalar dates, grade and kind, guessing and choosing the school, places, ties, one more net, outside the list, averages), the Artış view (totals, increases against Türkiye, the increase ranking, Önceki → Şimdiki), charts, the Sıra view, the viewing teacher and privacy.
- `test/turkiye-regression.cjs` also covers the T method: Φ and Φ⁻¹, recovering N and σ from synthetic order statistics, the range-restriction correction on a bivariate-normal population and its fallbacks (`normal` option of the fixture), the pooled N, a hand T and place example, real place inside the list against an estimate outside it, a gap without the school's net, the total and the T view.
- `test/turkiye-t-browser.cjs` runs the default Dilim ve sıra view at 1280 and 390 px, light and dark: the toggle and the all-lines chart (a line per subject and the bold total, hover, click to isolate), the KPI strip, the range chips, the schools table (sorting, the pinned school, the jump, "—" for a school in one exam) and switching to Artış and Sıra.
- The regression test also covers complete lists, the ΔT ranking (ties, a school missing from a list with "≈"), a mix of complete and truncated exams, the range chips and the table.
- `test/turkiye-browser.cjs` runs on desktop (light theme) and at 390 px (dark theme). It covers the menu, PDF ekle, choosing the school, the Artış view (a subject's increase and ranking, the TYT total chart and its readout), a third exam moving the chart, hover and keyboard, line highlight and click, a subject's net chart and nearby schools, TYT/AYT, Önceki, Çizgi, no sideways scrolling, and deleting.
- The synthetic report is in `test/fixtures/kurum-pdf.cjs`; it holds no real school data.
